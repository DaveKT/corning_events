How to subscribe

On iPhone or Mac, tap or click a Subscribe link and confirm. On Google Calendar, use Other calendars, From URL and paste the plain link. Google refreshes external calendars on its own schedule, often many hours behind.

Where this comes from

Events are collected from public calendars published by venues and regional organizations. Each entry credits its source and links back to it, so full details and tickets are one tap away. Nothing here is official: check with the organizer before making plans around an event.
